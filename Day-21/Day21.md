# Database Structure
This project uses three relational database tables.

# 1. Customers Table

Stores customer information.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| customer_id | Unique customer ID (Primary Key) |
| customer_name | Customer name |
| city | Customer location |

# 2. Products Table

Stores product information.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| product_id | Unique product ID (Primary Key) |
| product_name | Product name |
| category | Product category |
| price | Product selling price |

# 3. Orders Table

Stores transaction information.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| order_id | Unique order ID (Primary Key) |
| customer_id | Customer reference (Foreign Key) |
| product_id | Product reference (Foreign Key) |
| quantity | Number of products purchased |
| order_date | Date of purchase |

---

# Database Relationships

- Customers.customer_id → Orders.customer_id
- Products.product_id → Orders.product_id

The Orders table connects customers and products through Foreign Keys.

#  Business Problems Solved

### 1. Calculate Total Revenue

Calculated the total revenue generated by the company.

**Formula:**

Revenue = Product Price × Quantity

Used SUM(), JOIN, and GROUP BY-related logic.

### 2. Category-Wise Revenue Analysis

Identified which product category generated the highest revenue.

### 3. Top-Selling Products

Analyzed product-wise quantity sold and total revenue to identify top-performing products.

### 4. Top Customers Analysis

Identified customers contributing the highest revenue to the business.

### 5. Monthly Sales Analysis

Analyzed monthly revenue to identify the best-performing sales month.

### 6. High-Value Customers

Used HAVING to identify customers whose total spending exceeded ₹40,000.

### 7. Sales Performance Classification

Used CASE WHEN to classify orders into:

- High Value
- Medium Value
- Low Value

### 8. Average Order Value (AOV)

**Formula:**

AOV = Total Revenue / Total Orders

Calculated the average revenue generated per order.

### 9. Orders Per Customer

Identified how many orders each customer placed.

### 10. Products Without Sales

Used LEFT JOIN and IS NULL to identify products that have never been ordered.

## Key Business Insights

Based on the sample sales dataset, the following insights were identified:

| Business Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | ₹1,77,600 |
| Total Orders | 8 |
| Average Order Value | ₹22,200 |

# Important Findings

1. Electronics is the highest revenue-generating category.
2. Laptop sales contribute significantly to total revenue.
3. March recorded the highest monthly revenue.
4. Rahul is the highest revenue-generating customer.
5. Product quantity and product revenue are different performance indicators.
6. Customer purchasing behavior can help businesses develop better marketing strategies.

# Important Business Formulas

| Metric | Formula |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Selling Price × Quantity |
| Profit | Revenue − Total Cost |
| Profit Margin | (Profit / Revenue) × 100 |
| Average Order Value | Total Revenue / Total Orders |

**Note:** Profit and profit margin require product cost information, which is not included in the original sample dataset.
