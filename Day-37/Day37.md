# Day 37 - Power BI Dashboard Design and Formatting

A well-designed dashboard allows users and business managers to understand important information and make better decisions quickly.

## 1. Dashboard Design
Dashboard design is the process of arranging different visual elements such as charts, tables, cards, and slicers on a report page.

**Example:**
A Sales Dashboard may contain:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Region-wise Sales
- Product Performance

## 2. Dashboard Layout
Layout refers to how different visuals are arranged on a report page.

**Important Layout Practices:**
- Place important KPI cards at the top.
- Position charts in the middle.
- Display detailed tables at the bottom.
- Keep related visuals together.
- Maintain proper spacing and alignment.

A good layout makes reports easier to read and understand.

## 3. Formatting Visuals
Formatting means changing the appearance of visuals to make reports more attractive and understandable.

**Important Formatting Options:**

- **Title:** Adds meaningful names to charts and visuals.
- **Background:** Changes the background color of visuals.
- **Font:** Controls font style, size, and appearance.
- **Data Labels:** Displays numerical values directly on charts.
- **Borders:** Adds boundaries around visuals.
- **Colors:** Helps distinguish different categories and values.

**Example:**

Instead of using the title "Sum of Sales", use "Monthly Sales Performance".

## 4. Themes and Colors
Themes control the overall appearance of a Power BI report.

They help maintain consistent colors, fonts, and formatting throughout the report.

**Examples of Colors:**
- Blue – Professional business reports.
- Green – Growth and positive performance.
- Grey – Simple and clean dashboard design.

Using consistent colors makes dashboards look professional.

## 5. Slicers and Filters Design
Slicers are interactive visual elements that allow users to filter dashboard data based on selected values.

**Examples:**
- Year
- Month
- Region
- Product Category

**Example:**

If a sales manager selects the year 2025 in a Year slicer, the related visuals can display sales information for 2025.

Slicers help users explore data without changing the original dataset.

## 6. Dashboard Design Best Practices

Important practices for creating professional dashboards:

1. Keep the dashboard simple and clean.
2. Avoid using too many visuals.
3. Use readable fonts and suitable font sizes.
4. Maintain consistent colors.
5. Highlight important KPIs.
6. Use meaningful chart titles.
7. Align visuals properly.
8. Avoid unnecessary decorations.
9. Maintain proper spacing between visuals.

## 7. Practical Example – Sales Dashboard

A Sales Dashboard can be created using the following visuals:

| Visual | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Card | Display Total Sales |
| Card | Display Total Profit |
| Card | Display Total Orders |
| Line Chart | Show Monthly Sales Trends |
| Bar Chart | Compare Region-wise Sales |
| Pie Chart | Display Category-wise Sales |
| Slicer | Filter data by Year and Region |

This dashboard helps managers understand sales performance and identify business trends.
